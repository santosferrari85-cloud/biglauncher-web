Built for controllers, from the ground up

Every screen, popup and menu navigates just as well with an Xbox controller or a keyboard — without leaving the mouse behind if you prefer it. Button icons can also be shown in PlayStation or Nintendo style.

Recent games and Continue

Pick up your last game in two clicks, with a summary of what you've missed since you last played.

Memories Mode

After a while of inactivity, the app turns into an attraction mode that revisits your history: a timeline of games, highlighted stats, a yearly heatmap and your own screenshots.

Yearly stats

A "Wrapped"-style summary of your gaming year: total hours, favorite genres, achievements unlocked and completed games.

AI analysis (optional)

Summaries of a game's status, answers to free-form questions about your progress, and a short epilogue when you complete a game at 100%. Fully toggleable, and your identity or Steam account are never sent.

Ambient music

Each game's main theme plays in the background as you browse, with a dedicated player and support for your own soundtracks.

Community and Friends

Discussions, screenshots, artwork and reviews from Steam Community, plus an achievements and playtime comparison with your friends.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Valve or Steam product?

No. BigLauncher is an independent, non-profit project, not affiliated with Valve Corporation. "Steam" is a registered trademark of Valve.

Do I need a Steam API Key?

It's not required. Without one, BigLauncher still shows your installed games and works normally, but some features (full library, achievements, stats, friends) need a free API Key that you get from your Steam account and enter in Settings. If you're not sure where to find it, the Settings menu itself has a shortcut to get one.

Does it work with Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo controllers?

The Xbox controller is confirmed and tested. For PlayStation and Nintendo, BigLauncher can display button icons in that style from Settings, but it isn't guaranteed that the controller itself will work correctly — it depends on whether your system recognizes it as a standard gamepad. Keyboard always works.

What data is sent to the AI when it's enabled?

Only information about the game itself (name, genres, playtime, achievements, news). Your username, Steam ID and API Key are never sent. The feature is optional and disabled by default.

What languages is it available in?

Spanish, English, French and German.

How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?

Through the repository's Issues on GitHub.
